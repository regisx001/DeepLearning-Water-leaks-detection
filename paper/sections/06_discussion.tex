\section{Discussion}

\noindent The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed CNN-BiLSTM-Attention
architecture is effective for detecting water leaks in multivariate time-series
data. The model achieves strong discriminative performance and provides a
balanced trade-off between precision, recall, and false alarm rate, which is
critical for real-world monitoring systems.

\vspace{0.2cm}

\noindent The benchmark comparison highlights key differences between approaches.
While Random Forest achieves strong AUC-PR, it exhibits lower recall and F1-score
at the selected operating point. Logistic Regression performs competitively due
to feature engineering but lacks the ability to model temporal dependencies.
In contrast, the proposed model achieves the best overall balance, confirming
the importance of combining convolutional, recurrent, and attention-based
mechanisms.

\vspace{0.2cm}

\noindent Another important aspect is the use of threshold calibration, which allows
the model to adapt to different operational requirements by controlling the
trade-off between detection sensitivity and false alarms. This flexibility is
essential for practical deployment in real-world systems.

\vspace{0.2cm}

\noindent Despite these promising results, some limitations remain. The dataset is
restricted to a specific monitoring period, which may limit generalization.
Additionally, the benchmark comparison could be extended to include more models,
and the interpretability of the deep learning architecture remains limited.

\vspace{0.2cm}

\noindent Overall, the results confirm that hybrid deep learning architectures are
well-suited for time-series leak detection, particularly when combined with
robust preprocessing and evaluation strategies.
