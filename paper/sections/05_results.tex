\section{Results}

\subsection{Training Performance}

Training curves from the best-performing fold are shown in Figure~\ref{fig:training}.
The model converges within 15--30 epochs, with early stopping preventing overfitting
on the small training set.

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{images/training_curves.png}
\caption{Training and validation loss/accuracy curves.}
\label{fig:training}
\end{figure}

Training accuracy typically reaches 90--95\% within the first 10 epochs, while
validation accuracy plateaus at a lower level. This gap is expected: the model sees
only 8 sensors during training and must generalize to 2 entirely unseen sensors with
potentially different leak characteristics.

\subsection{Threshold Tuning}

The threshold sensitivity analysis (Figure~\ref{fig:threshold}) for the best-performing
fold shows how the F1-score varies with the decision boundary.

\begin{figure}[H]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{images/threshold_tuning.png}
\caption{F1-Score as a function of decision threshold.}
\label{fig:threshold}
\end{figure}

In practice, the optimal threshold depends on the \textbf{application requirements}: in
safety-critical leak detection, a lower threshold (higher recall, more false alarms) may
be preferable, while in cost-sensitive deployments a higher threshold is appropriate.

 & & $\pm$0.242 & $\pm$0.372 & $\pm$0.400 & $\pm$0.370 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Fold 1} (S001, S009): Achieves perfect scores. Both sensors have
    multiple leak events with clear temporal patterns.
    \item \textbf{Fold 2} (S002, S007): The worst-performing fold. The model fails to
    correctly identify the leak pattern, predicting everything as normal.
    \item \textbf{Fold 3} (S006, S010): Near-perfect performance. S010 has the most
    diverse leak patterns (5 leak events).
    \item \textbf{Fold 5} (S003, S004): Poor performance. S003 has \textbf{zero leaks},
    causing the model to over-predict leaks on its normal windows.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Summary of Results}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Accuracy}: 71.30\%
    \item \textbf{Precision}: 74.07\%
    \item \textbf{Recall}: 85.71\%
    \item \textbf{F1-Score}: 0.79
    \item \textbf{True Positives}: 60 out of 70 leak windows detected
    \item \textbf{False Positives}: 21 out of 38 normal windows misclassified
    \item \textbf{Per-fold variance}: Accuracy ranges from 33.3\% to 100\% across folds
\end{itemize}
