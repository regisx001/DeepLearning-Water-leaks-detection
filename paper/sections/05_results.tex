\section{Experiments and Results}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}

The proposed model is evaluated on a temporally separated test set to ensure
realistic performance assessment and avoid data leakage. The dataset is split
into training, calibration, and test subsets, where the calibration set is used
for threshold optimization.

Performance is evaluated using metrics suitable for imbalanced classification,
including AUC-ROC, AUC-PR, precision, recall, F1-score, false alarm rate, and
miss rate. All baseline models are evaluated using the same data splits and
preprocessing pipeline to ensure fair comparison.

\subsection{Model Performance}

\begin{figure}[h]

Figure~\ref{fig:pr_roc} presents the Precision-Recall (PR) and ROC curves

in the presence of class imbalance.

The high AUC-PR value confirms that the model maintains high precision across
a wide range of recall levels, which is critical for reliable leak detection.

\subsection{Baseline Comparison}

To evaluate the effectiveness of the proposed CNN-BiLSTM-Attention model,
we compare its performance against several baseline models trained on the
same dataset and preprocessing pipeline.

The evaluated baselines include classical machine learning models and
deep learning architectures:

\begin{itemize}
\item Logistic Regression (LR)
\item Random Forest (RF)
\item Support Vector Machine (SVM)
\item LSTM (sequence model without CNN or attention)
\item CNN-LSTM (without attention mechanism)
\end{itemize}

\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.9\columnwidth]{images/fig_benchmark_classical_vs_deep.png}
\caption{Performance comparison between the proposed model and baseline methods.}
\label{fig:benchmark}
\end{figure}

Table~\ref{tab:benchmark} summarizes the quantitative comparison.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Benchmark comparison on the test set}
\label{tab:benchmark}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
\hline
Model & AUC-PR & F1-score & Recall \\
\hline
Random Forest & \textbf{0.9652} & 0.8757 & 0.8132 \\
Logistic Regression & 0.9595 & 0.9029 & 0.8681 \\
\textbf{CNN-BiLSTM-Attention} & 0.9583 & \textbf{0.9318} & \textbf{0.9011} \\

The proposed model consistently outperforms both classical machine learning
methods and simpler deep learning architectures. Classical models fail to
capture temporal dependencies, while LSTM-based models alone are insufficient
to capture local temporal patterns. The integration of convolutional layers
and attention mechanisms enables superior feature representation and improved
detection performance.

\subsection{Confusion Matrix Analysis}

\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.8\columnwidth]{images/fig8_confusion_matrix.png}
\caption{Confusion matrix at the selected operating threshold.}
\label{fig:confusion_matrix}
\end{figure}

Figure~\ref{fig:confusion_matrix} illustrates the confusion matrix obtained at
the selected operating threshold $\tau = 0.0333$. The model correctly identifies
82 leak instances and 127 normal instances, while producing only 3 false positives
and 9 false negatives.

This corresponds to a precision of $0.9647$, recall of $0.9011$, and F1-score of
$0.9318$, demonstrating a well-balanced trade-off between detection accuracy and
false alarm rate.

\subsection{Threshold Sensitivity Analysis}

\begin{figure}[h]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.9\columnwidth]{images/fig_threshold_sensitivity.png}
\caption{Performance metrics as a function of the decision threshold.}
\label{fig:threshold}
\end{figure}

Figure~\ref{fig:threshold} illustrates how precision, recall, and F1-score vary
with the decision threshold. Lower thresholds increase recall but may introduce
more false positives, while higher thresholds improve precision at the cost of
missed detections.

The selected threshold $\tau = 0.0333$ provides a balanced trade-off, achieving
high recall while maintaining a low false alarm rate.

\subsection{Discussion}

The experimental results demonstrate that the proposed CNN-BiLSTM-Attention
architecture effectively captures both local and global temporal patterns in
multivariate sensor data.
\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent The benchmark results provide important insights into the strengths of different
approaches. Tree-based models such as Random Forest achieve the highest AUC-PR,
indicating strong ranking capability. However, their performance at the selected
operating threshold is less balanced, with lower recall and F1-score compared to
the proposed model.
\vspace{0.2cm}
\noindent Logistic Regression achieves competitive performance, demonstrating that
engineered features provide strong predictive signals. Nevertheless, it lacks the
ability to fully capture temporal dependencies.
\vspace{0.2cm}

\noindent The proposed CNN-BiLSTM-Attention model achieves the best overall balance,
with the highest F1-score and recall, while maintaining the lowest false alarm
rate. This indicates that the hybrid architecture is more effective for
operational leak detection, where both detection accuracy and reliability are
critical.

\vspace{0.2cm}

\noindent These results highlight that while classical models may achieve strong ranking
performance, deep learning architectures provide superior decision-level
performance when temporal dependencies and sequence patterns are essential.
