\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This work implemented a full deep learning pipeline for water leak detection,
from preprocessing and feature engineering to sequence modeling and visual
evaluation. The CNN-BiLSTM-attention model trained with binary cross-entropy
achieved AUC-ROC 0.6364 and AUC-PR 0.5008 on the current test split.

At the selected threshold ($\tau=0.0913$), the detector prioritizes leak
capture (recall 1.0000) but produces many false alarms (precision 0.2821,
accuracy 0.2821). The generated PR/ROC, confusion-matrix, score-distribution,
and threshold-sensitivity figures show that threshold policy is the dominant
factor for practical performance.

Overall, the study confirms that sequence deep learning is viable for this
task, but operational success requires explicit threshold design aligned with
field constraints. Future iterations should focus on robust threshold
optimization, temporal cross-validation, and model export improvements for
stable deployment.
